Cases, appointments and status, in one place.

The problem

Health information can become fragmented across appointments, case notes, and follow-up schedules, making day-to-day case tracking harder than it needs to be.

The approach

A focused desktop workflow combines patient profiles, case status, scheduling, and an appointment calendar so information stays visible and actionable.

What it keeps track of.

Patient case management

Profiles and case notes for each mother and child, with their current status always visible.

Appointment scheduling

An appointment calendar for check-ups and follow-ups, so nothing slips between visits.

Longitudinal status tracking

Status changes recorded over time, so health workers can see how each case is progressing.
